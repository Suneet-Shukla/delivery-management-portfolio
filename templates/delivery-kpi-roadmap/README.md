# Delivery KPI and Roadmap Tracker

## Purpose

This Excel workbook provides a structured approach to tracking roadmap delivery, release readiness, risks, dependencies, discovery governance and work-item quality.

## Workbook Contents

### Roadmap Delivery

Tracks committed dates, current forecasts, actual completion dates, delivery status, delays, blockers, ownership and delivery interventions.

### Release Readiness

Tracks testing, security, monitoring, alerts, rollback planning, operational support, go/no-go approval and post-release reviews.

### RAID

Tracks risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies, including ownership, impact, mitigation, escalation and resolution.

### Discovery Gate

Confirms that product discovery, experiments, evidence, success measures and approvals are completed before development begins.

### ADO Hygiene

Measures the completeness and quality of delivery work items, including parent relationships, estimates, financial classification and time data.

## Delivery Problems Addressed

- Lack of consolidated roadmap visibility
- Unclear ownership of delivery risks
- Inconsistent release-readiness checks
- Development beginning before discovery is complete
- Missing or incomplete work-item information
- Limited evidence of delivery-management interventions
