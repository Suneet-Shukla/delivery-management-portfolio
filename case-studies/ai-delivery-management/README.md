# AI-Assisted Delivery Management

> This is an illustrative case study using fictional and sanitized information.

## Scenario

A delivery programme includes multiple teams, shared dependencies, changing priorities and weekly leadership reporting. Delivery information is distributed across work items, dashboards, meeting notes and RAID logs.

The Delivery Manager needs to identify risks early, improve backlog quality and provide leadership with a reliable view of delivery confidence.

## Delivery Challenges

- Inconsistent backlog readiness across teams
- Dependencies identified late in the delivery cycle
- Significant manual effort required for weekly reporting
- Status reports describing activity without highlighting decisions
- Risks and actions distributed across multiple meetings and tools
- Limited visibility into aging work and delivery confidence

## AI-Assisted Approach

| Delivery Activity | AI Assistance | Delivery Manager Accountability |
|---|---|---|
| Backlog readiness | Identify missing outcomes, acceptance criteria, owners and dependencies | Validate findings and agree actions with the team |
| Risk management | Highlight aging work, repeated blockers and schedule risks | Assess impact and determine mitigation |
| Dependency management | Compare plans and identify cross-team conflicts | Confirm ownership and escalation path |
| Leadership reporting | Draft concise summaries from approved delivery information | Verify accuracy, and communicate decisions |
| Meeting follow-up | Extract actions, owners, dates and unresolved questions | Confirm commitments with participants |
| Delivery forecasting | Identify trends that may affect milestone confidence | Apply judgement and update the delivery plan |

## Example Workflow

1. Export approved delivery information from the system of record.
2. Remove or mask confidential and personal information.
3. Ask AI to identify gaps, risks, contradictions and overdue actions.
4. Validate the findings with delivery and engineering teams.
5. Record confirmed actions in the appropriate delivery system.
6. Produce a concise leadership update.
7. Track whether recommended actions improve the delivery outcome.

## Responsible AI Controls

- Use only organisation-approved AI tools
- Do not submit confidential or personal information
- Treat AI output as a recommendation rather than a fact
- Validate summaries and risks and forecasts against source data
- Maintain human ownership of prioritisation and escalation decisions
- Record important decisions in the official system of record
- Review AI outputs for bias, omissions and unsupported conclusions

## Expected Benefits

- Faster preparation of delivery reports
- Earlier identification of risks and dependencies
- More consistent backlog-readiness reviews
- Clearer meeting actions and ownership
- Greater focus on decisions and outcomes
- Improved traceability across delivery activities

## Limitations

AI output depends on the completeness and quality of the information provided. It may miss context, make unsupported assumptions or incorrectly interpret status. The Delivery Manager remains accountable for validation, communication and decision-making.

## Measures of Success

- Reduction in weekly reporting preparation time
- Percentage of features meeting readiness criteria
- Number of dependencies identified before development begins
- Aging of blocked work items
- Percentage of actions completed by their target dates
- Milestone predictability
- Stakeholder satisfaction with delivery reporting
